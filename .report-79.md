## Fixture contract (what the driver actually exercises)

- The REAL `canter` binary (`CARGO_BIN_EXE_canter`), the REAL daemon
  (`canter daemon run`) over a per-test Unix socket, the real SQLite state
  under a temp `XDG_STATE_HOME`, and the debug-only crash hook
  (`CANTER_CRASH_POINT`, honored only in debug builds).
- The ONLY adapter is one fake `herdr` shell script on the daemon's
  allowlisted adapter PATH. It records every invocation
  (`session start` / `session show` / `session interrupt`) and its read-backs
  are switched per phase (`ready` / `orchestrator` / `reused-process` /
  `no-process` / `malformed` / `binding-match|fallback|unexpected` and source
  modes `live` / `reused-source` / retired). Invocation assertions are
  delta- or filter-based, never absolute.
- Synthetic identities only (`sess-0001`, `sess-0002`, `proc-0001`,
  `worktrees/issues/79`, `lane-79-*`); no host paths, no secret material, no
  network, no credential, no live provider/model change.
- The profile-switch fixture runs the real `canter config show --json`
  decoder on a temp-dir config (fictional `example-*` provider/model names)
  under an environment with NO credential set, and asserts the credential
  report says so (`credentials.missing == ["EXAMPLE_PROVIDER_KEY"]`).

## Fixture proof vs real-adapter/provider proof (explicit separation)

**Certified by this driver (fixture-level):** the real daemon/state/socket/
SQLite behavior for the whole merged handoff chain — replacement records,
checkpoint capture + derived artifact reconciliation, guarded retirement,
successor start/adoption, completion consumption, the shown refusal codes and
their before-any-effect ordering, restart reconciliation of the four
interrupted transition kinds (no duplicate spawn, no repeated signal, no
duplicate owner), pause stickiness, and the profile-binding documents produced
by the real config decoder.

**NOT certified — explicit remaining gates (real adapter/provider):**

1. The real `herdr` (workspace) CLI/json contract: the fixture is a shell
   script. Real `session start/show/interrupt` response parsing, readiness
   and registration/release semantics are covered only by that fixture here.
2. Real PID/pane identity and reuse: process identities are synthetic
   strings; no real process/pane table is consulted.
3. Real provider/model introspection: `binding_introspection` read-backs are
   synthesized by the fixture; no provider API, no credential, no live
   model binding is exercised. The measured `actual` binding proves the
   plumbing, not provider support.
4. Real daemon supervision: the daemon is spawned per test and killed; no
   service manager/launchd behavior is exercised.
5. Artifact rot AFTER a capture claim has resolved: the registry regenerates
   the derived brief during restart reconciliation of an in-flight capture
   (and fails closed on an orphan), but nothing re-verifies a resolved
   checkpoint artifact at rest — the durable row stays authoritative and the
   start/retire digest fences still refuse changed evidence.
